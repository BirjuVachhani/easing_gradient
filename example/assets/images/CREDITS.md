## osaka.webp

Osaka Castle, photographed by Wikimedia Commons user
[Syced](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Syced).

Source: [File:Osaka castle 9.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osaka_castle_9.jpg)

License: [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en).
No attribution is required; this credit is here because it is the decent thing
to do.

Resized to 900x1200 and re-encoded as WebP for the example app. The photo was
chosen for its large area of smooth sky: a gradient mask fading across an even
tone is where uneven alpha pacing becomes visible, which is the point the
landing page demo is making.
